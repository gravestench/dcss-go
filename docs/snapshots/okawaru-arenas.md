# Okawaru Arena text snapshots

Generated by `TestOkawaruArenaTextSnapshots` from the four committed DES
maps on the real 80×70 staging grid. The view is cropped to each map's
metal-wall enclosure. `A` is the player landing, `T` is the challenged
monster landing, `B` is the sealed wall that opens after victory, `E` is the
return arch, and `S` is a generated spectator. `#` denotes a wall and `'`
an open doorway.

The route in each layout runs from `A`, past `T`, through `B` after the
opponent's final death, and down to `E`. Climbing `E` returns the player to
their exact Dungeon departure cell and retires the temporary Arena floor.

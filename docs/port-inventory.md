Content import policy for the whole Go port: every imported DCSS content category is generated into a reviewable artifact committed to this repository. Category-specific importers are optional developer tools for intentional upstream content refreshes. Normal Go builds, tests, CI, saves, and gameplay read committed Go-repository content; none runs an importer or requires the C++ checkout. A content refresh updates the artifact and its source provenance in a reviewed commit.

Run the slice with `go run ./cmd/dcrawl -seed 1 -save dcrawl-save.json` from this repository. On startup, choose Dungeon Crawl or the saved-run entry. Hill Orc (`a`), Minotaur (`b`), Human (`j`), or Deep Elf (`p`), Fighter (`a`), and weapons (`a`–`f`) are the playable choices; enter a name before or after those choices. During play, move with `h/j/k/l/y/u/b/n` or arrows, wait with `.` or `s`, pick up with `g` or `,`, drop with `d` then an optional quantity and inventory letter, wear with `W` or take off with `T` and the inventory letter (Esc interrupts the delay), take stairs with `<` or `>`, open inventory with `i`, skills with `m`, quaff carried curing or might with `q` then its inventory letter, and open help with `?`. In the skills menu, `/` changes automatic/manual training, `|` changes toggle/focus action, and a skill letter applies that action. `S` confirms save and exit, Ctrl-S saves and exits immediately, and Ctrl-Q confirms abandonment. `L` means run right and currently explains that it is unavailable. Menus, notices, unsupported potion effects, and resizes do not spend auts.

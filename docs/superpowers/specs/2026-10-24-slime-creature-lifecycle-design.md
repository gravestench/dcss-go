# Slime Creature Lifecycle Design

## Intent

Ordinary slime creatures must split and merge while pursuing a foe, not
merely spawn at a preselected size. The source behavior makes a group of
small threats become one strong body when its route is blocked, and lets a
large body divide when it cannot close on its target. This matters for
combat, save/restore, and Okawaru's Arena Duel lineage.

## Constraints

- Keep gameplay policy in readable, commented Lua. Go owns generic ECS and
  grid services only. No inline Lua in Go.
- Keep one ECS entity per living slime body. `monster.slime_blob.size` is the
  number of merged bodies, capped at five. A size-one body may omit it.
- Use the pinned DCSS `mon-abil.cc` split/merge rules as the behavioral
  reference. Existing Jiyva jelly feeding split is a separate rule.
- Preserve deterministic replay independent of file load order, and use a
  named RNG stream for the new decision and fair placement.
- Work on the current Go `main`; merge this contiguous category as one commit
  and push it after verification.

## Approaches considered

1. **Recommended: focused Lua lifecycle policy called from monster decision.**
   It reads live ECS state and the grid, changes the two bodies, and reports
   whether an action was spent. This follows the existing ugly-thing natural
   ability seam without extending Go's monster-specific dispatch.
2. A new Go ECS system could implement the same decisions. It would need
   monster-specific logic and Lua policy adapters in Go and weaken modding.
3. A generic merge/split component and resolver could support future species,
   but their rules differ and the abstraction would hide the source behavior.

The first approach best keeps the owner small and discoverable. This is an
implementation call based on the existing Lua-first design.

## Behavior

- Only actual slime creatures can use the ability. A shapeshifter in slime
  form cannot. Paralysis, confusion, and fleeing suppress both operations.
- Try split first. A body needs size above one, at least four current HP,
  and a successful one-in-two roll. If it sees a foe more than one cell away,
  a clear adjacent cell nearer the foe takes priority over division. Otherwise
  choose a free, habitable adjacent site no farther from the visible foe,
  fairly among eligible cells.
- Split the blob count as `floor(size / 2)` for the new child, leaving the
  other blobs on the parent. Scale each body's max and current HP by its new
  share, truncating like the source. Copy pursuit, allegiance, active effects,
  summon ownership, and current Arena Duel lineage where meaningful. The
  child is an independently scheduled ECS actor. The split spends the parent's
  action and must not give the child an immediate extra action.
- If split does not occur, a pursuing slime may merge into an adjacent
  compatible slime only when that square shortens distance to its target and
  no open adjacent square also shortens that distance. Combined size cannot
  exceed five. Compatibility includes actual species, allegiance, summon
  status, and incompatible control effects. Pool max/current HP, retain the
  target body, transfer the active Duel mark, and remove the consumed body
  without a death reward or corpse. If a child absorbs the original body,
  carry its population or vault origin into the survivor. Charge the survivor
  an action.
- Name and attack damage continue to use the existing size owner. Visible
  splits and merges emit source-shaped messages.
- Persist a small birth marker only when a new body is generated by a split.
  It admits that body outside the original roster budget and validates its
  species, required actor state, and birth time. Polymorph removes the old
  blob size but keeps this roster origin. Because split rounding and merges
  between authored bodies can change HP per blob, the saved blob component
  records the resulting maximum. Save validation bounds that value by the
  larger of the live species profile and source-authored fixed HP present in
  that level's vault records. For a Jiyva-fed slime the source's four-times-
  average ceiling applies per blob. It checks the recorded maximum exactly
  unless Jiyva has since fed the body; Jiyva's lineage then validates further
  growth. A polymorphed slime uses its live HP profile.
  Planerend summons retain their source after division, and save validation
  accepts their source-shaped smaller bodies.

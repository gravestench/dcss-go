# Jiyva Jelly Ecology Design

## Purpose

Complete the connected Jiyva slime behavior in the pinned DCSS source:
neutral slimes look for edible floor items, eat before an ordinary action,
heal and split, and obey authored `no_jiyva` cells. Jiyva also creates
jellies at the source's periodic check and sometimes consumes items on a
visited floor. This is one domain slice because the same item eligibility
and cell property must govern movement, eating, spawning, and off-floor
effects. An implementation that only stores `KPROP` would not make the map
directive meaningful during play.

The current conversion, allegiance, penance, and Slime:5 seal owners remain
authoritative. New behavior is Lua policy and Lua systems over saved Akara
components and the existing level grid. Go may expose generic read-only
level enumeration if the off-floor rule needs it, but Go must not name Jiyva,
jellies, item kinds, or vault properties.

## Source behavior

The pinned `mon-behv.cc`, `mon-movetarget.cc`, `mon-act.cc`, `god-passive.cc`,
`timed-effects.cc`, `item-prop.cc`, and `slime.des` define this category.

- A Jiyva worshipper in good standing has the zero-rank neutral slime,
  jelly army, and jelly eating passives. Penance suspends their effects.
- A good-neutral slime seeks a visible edible item inside the source search
  radius, skipping `no_jiyva` cells. Its ordinary targeting remains when
  there is no eligible item.
- At the start of a slime's monster action, an awake, unsubmerged slime on
  an allowed cell can eat floor items. Eating consumes that action. The
  source rolls a 1–10 unit budget, consumes at most that many nongold units
  and at most 50 healing points, and treats a gold stack as one budget unit.
  Gold above 500 uses its source bounded reduction before healing. Partly
  eaten stacks remain with their exact saved quantity; fully eaten entities
  are destroyed through the ordinary item lifecycle.
- Nets fixed in place, artefacts, the Horn of Geryon, ziggurat figurines,
  mimics, the Orb, and runes are inedible. Other source item classes,
  including ordinary gold, are eligible. The same predicate applies to
  active and off-floor eating.
- Eating heals the slime by at least one and at most 50 HP, capped by the
  source's four-times-average and monster maximum. A species with `M_SPLITS`
  may divide at its source HP threshold. The child retains the parent's
  live species and allegiance, appears in a legal adjacent cell, and gets
  a source-timed energy offset. The parent and child hold independent saved
  actor, health, energy, position, and allegiance state.
- Each source Jiyva timer fires after a saved random 100–300 aut interval.
  On that event, it has a one-in-ten chance to try one
  through five good-neutral jellies on legal floor or shallow-water cells.
  Occupied, clouded, and `no_jiyva` cells are ineligible. It emits one of
  the source messages when at least one appears and the player is not
  silenced. The one-in-25 off-floor item check is independent.
- The off-floor check uses only previously visited, inactive levels. Its
  source bounded rejection draws can end without eating. Eligible items
  use the same inedible filter and `no_jiyva` protection; destruction and
  the distant slurping message affect saved level state. No loaded level
  may be created or regenerated by the check.
- `KPROP: ... = no_jiyva` is already saved as per-cell typed vault records.
  This slice interprets that record at play time. The Slime:5 fixup clears
  the effective restriction in its released inner region; unrelated maps,
  such as Aquarium helpers, keep theirs. Source property names come from a
  shared Lua constants module.

## Owners and runtime flow

`policy/religion/gods/jiyva.lua` remains the entry point for worship state
and slime allegiance. Focused files beneath `policy/religion/jiyva/` own
item eligibility, allowed cells and targets, feeding and splitting, and
periodic effects. Each function documents why its source rule exists and
how it reads or mutates the relevant ECS state. The named per-cell property
reader scans saved vault records, ignoring overridden records; it is not a
Go switch over property strings.

Monster decision calls the feeding owner after incapacity checks and before
ordinary spell, attack, or movement selection. A successful feed writes a
wait intent and returns. Good-neutral movement consults the item-target
owner before generic wandering. These are the only integration calls in
the already large monster decision module.

One player-time Lua system owns the source's randomly spaced checks. A saved
player component holds the next due aut, so long actions, save/restore, and
repeated scheduler visits cannot duplicate or omit checks. It rolls only
through a named game RNG stream. The off-floor service reads the existing
visited levels and their live item entities; it never alters an unvisited
branch or keeps mutable Lua module state.

The Slime seal owner exposes whether its released markers have lifted that
map's `no_jiyva` restriction. This is a domain query over saved markers,
not a second source of the seal state. If a level has no Slime seal markers,
its authored `no_jiyva` records remain in force.

## Mod behavior and validation

A mod can add `no_jiyva` to any `.des` map without changing Go. A mod-added
monster whose source genus and `M_SPLITS` flag are registered uses the same
Jiyva rules. A mod-added item uses the same class and special-identity
predicate. Unknown or malformed property records are left to the existing
vault validation; the ecology owner does not reinterpret another property.

Lua `_test.lua` cases use production schemas and helpers. They cover
edible and protected stacks, partial consumption and gold, no-Jiyva cells,
the Slime:5 release, neutral item pursuit, incapacitation, healing and
splitting, periodic spawn exclusions, and off-floor destruction. A Go
integration test traverses an actual generated Slime floor, verifies the
released seal and next ecology draw, and restores the resulting jelly.
Separate production scheduler tests feed a live endoplasm, keep a partial
item stack, and restore its growth state. The category ends with focused tests, a broad Go
build/test check, style inspection of changed files, and a single commit.

## Explicit boundary

Jiyva's mutation gifts, corrosion protection, regeneration bonus, wrath,
slime-wall immunity, and five-star retaliatory slimes are separate religion
action and passive categories. This slice owns the three related zero-rank
slime ecology passives and every `no_jiyva` consumer they use.

The current actor model does not expose a submerged state or the parent's
split XP accounting. These source checks remain named fidelity gaps until
their state owners are ported.
